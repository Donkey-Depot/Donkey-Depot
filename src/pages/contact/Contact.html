OVERALL TONE

Ambitious. Technical. Local. Human.

  • Ambitious

    You're building something bigger than a normal recycling initiative.

  • Technical

    EcoLoop and the infrastructure side should feel genuinely engineered.

  • Local

    Juja and the surrounding community are central.

  • Human

    The team and community shouldn't disappear behind corporate language.

  • Forward-looking

    Much of the project is still being built, so the journey should feel exciting.

  • Honest

    Clearly distinguish what's operational, being developed, being piloted, and envisioned.

ABOUT THE PLATFORM

One Ecosystem for a Circular Future

EcoLoop is a digital circular-economy platform under development by Donkey Depot. Its purpose is to make recyclable materials easier to identify, collect, transport, and connect with appropriate recycling and processing facilities.

By bringing different participants together, EcoLoop aims to create a more connected and organized recycling ecosystem.

Currently in Development

EcoLoop's platform and services are currently under development. The features described on this page represent the planned direction of the platform and are not presented as currently operational services.

WHO IS ECOLOOP FOR?

Connecting Every Part of the Recycling Journey

EcoLoop is being designed for the people and organizations that make recycling and material recovery possible.

Households & Individuals

Individuals and families who want to manage recyclable household materials and participate in a more sustainable environment.

Businesses & Organizations

Shops, companies, offices, and organizations looking for better ways to organize and manage recyclable materials generated through their activities.

Transportation Providers

Truck owners, transport companies, and independent vehicle operators who can help move recyclable materials from collection points to recycling facilities.

Recycling Partners

Recycling facilities, material recovery centers, sorting operations, and processing companies that handle recyclable materials.

THE USER JOURNEY

How EcoLoop Is Designed to Work

A connected journey from identifying recyclable materials to their collection, transportation, and processing.

01. Register

Create an account and join the EcoLoop ecosystem.

02. Identify Materials

Identify recyclable materials and provide information about what needs to be collected.

03. Arrange Transport

Connect with suitable transportation providers to move recyclable materials.

04. Processing

Deliver materials to appropriate recycling, sorting, or processing facilities.

05. Environmental Impact

Support a more measurable and organized circular-economy system.

This describes the intended EcoLoop user journey. Its services are still under development.
